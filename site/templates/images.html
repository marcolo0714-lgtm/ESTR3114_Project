Reading the predictions

  • Both models receive exactly the same RGB image, resized to 32 × 32 with bilinear interpolation and normalized using training-only statistics. Resizing changes the aspect ratio of a non-square image.
  • The five probabilities cover only the selected superclass and sum to 100% before rounding. The largest is the predicted class.
  • These models were trained on small CIFAR-100 images. Large photographs, unfamiliar objects or an incorrect superclass can produce unreliable predictions, even with high probabilities.
  • The first request loads the selected checkpoints; later requests reuse them. Changing the checkpoint may require another first load.
  • One example does not establish which method is better. Read the settings and validation results.
